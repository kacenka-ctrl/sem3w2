# Business Law: Forms of Business Organisation Sorting Exercise

An interactive, responsive classroom starter exercise for Business Law students. Students sort 25 commercial and statutory characteristics into three core entity forms: **Sole Proprietorship**, **Partnership**, and **Limited Company (Private / Public)**.

## ✨ Features

- **Self-Contained & Lightweight:** Built in a single `index.html` file using Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript—zero dependencies, build steps, or servers required.
- **Interactive Flashcard Stack:** Cards appear in a 3D tactile deck; students can drag & drop cards or use one-tap category assign buttons on mobile/tablet devices.
- **Embedded Legal Glossary:** Hovering over (or tapping) key terms like *jointly and severally*, *perpetual succession*, or *separate legal entity* pops up contextual legal notes.
- **Assessment & Breakdown:** Instant calculation of student accuracy percentages, along with an itemized breakdown explaining legal distinctions.
- **Randomized Every Launch:** Cards are automatically shuffled using the Fisher-Yates algorithm on every page load and reset.

## 📋 Curriculum Matrix (25 Characteristics)

| Dimension | Sole Proprietorship | Partnership | Limited Company |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ownership** | 1 individual owner | 2+ partners (general or limited) | Shareholders (family/friends or general public) |
| **Liability** | 100% unlimited personal liability | General partners liable jointly & severally; limited partners capped | Limited to the amount unpaid on shares |
| **Capital** | Owner capital & personal loans | Partner equity & personal loans | Issuing shares & debentures; public listing on Stock Exchange |
| **Formation** | Informal start; no incorporation | Formed by agreement (oral, written, implied); no incorporation | Formal statutory incorporation at Companies House; Memorandum & Articles |
| **Governance** | Owner controls decisions & profits; may employ staff | Managed by partners; profit shared equally or by agreement | Corporate structure: Shareholders, Directors, Officers, Supervisory Board |
| **Termination** | Ends on owner's death or decision to close | Dissolved on death, withdrawal, or contract terms | Perpetual succession; wound up voluntarily or by court order |
